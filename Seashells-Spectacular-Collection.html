Seashells Spectacular Art Collection by Ben and Raisa Gertsberg

This collection was inspired by the beauty of Southern California coast and the joy of coastal living. Images from this gallery were selected by interior designers to decorate The Sunset Magazine 2005 Idea House. Multiple images from this gallery decorate The Sofia Hotel in San Diego, CA; South Coast Medical Center in Laguna Beach, CA; a luxury apartment building, The Colony at Fashion Island in Newport Beach, CA; an upscale office building in Aliso Viejo, CA; and many other residential and commercial Southern California properties that feature coastal decor, beach cottage decor, nautical decor and tropical decor.

Each artwork in this spectacular gallery may be purchased as a premium canvas print, framed print, metal print, acrylic print, wood print, or classic poster. Every order is backed by a 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee.
